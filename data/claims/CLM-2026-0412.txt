Claim Intake Narrative for CLM-2026-0412

The claimant, James Whitfield, is seeking assistance with repair costs resulting from a motor vehicle collision that occurred on April 12, 2026. According to the incident report, Mr. Whitfield was involved in a rear-end collision with another vehicle stopped at a red light on Route 9. The other vehicle was not reported.

The estimated repair cost for these damages is $6,200. However, as per policy terms, a collision deductible applies to this claim.

A medical note (MED-0412-A) from the clinic confirms his injuries and provides a record of the treatment he received. The details of Mr. Whitfield's physical condition will be further investigated as part of this claim.

The claimant has provided the necessary documentation, including the incident report, repair estimate, and medical records. This information will serve as the basis for the review and evaluation of his claim in accordance with policy number AU-3387-1.
