HI-6614-2 HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY

DECLARATIONS

THE OKAFOR FAMILY (POLICYHOLDER)declares that the information contained in this policy is accurate and true to the best of their knowledge. The Policyholder acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agreed to the terms and conditions of this policy.

COVERAGE SUMMARY

This Health Insurance Policy ("Policy") is designed to provide coverage for medical expenses and related costs incurred by the Policyholder and their covered family members. The Policy covers eligible medical expenses arising from normal risks associated with the Policyholder's life and health.

DEDUCTIBLES AND LIMITS

Schedule of Deductibles and Limits:

- Primary Care: $4000
- Specialist Care: $2000
- Emergency Room: $0 (covered in full)

LIMITS OF LIABILITY

The liability limit for this policy is $ Unlimited.

COINSURANCE

Coinsurance after deductible is 20% of eligible expenses.

COPAYMENTS

Primary Care: $25 per visit
Specialist Care: $50 per visit
Emergency Room: $300 per visit (coinsurance applies)

EXCLUSIONS

This Policy excludes coverage for the following services:

- Cosmetic procedures
- Experimental or investigational treatment
- Injuries sustained during illegal activity

DEFINITIONS

For the purpose of this Policy, the following terms are defined as follows:

- Deductible: The amount that the Policyholder must pay out-of-pocket for eligible medical expenses before the insurance coverage kicks in.
- Coinsurance: The percentage of eligible expenses that the Policyholder pays after meeting the deductible.
- Dwelling Coverage is not applicable to this policy type.
