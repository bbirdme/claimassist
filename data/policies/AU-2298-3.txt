AU-2298-3 AUTO INSURANCE POLICY

DECLARATIONS

1. The policyholder, Marcus Webb, hereby declares that:
   a) This is an auto insurance policy issued to cover the risks associated with the vehicle described below.

2. The vehicle covered by this policy is a 2017 Ford F-150, registered in [insert registration number], which is owned by the policyholder and used for personal purposes only.

3. The policyholder certifies that all information provided in this policy is true and accurate to the best of their knowledge.

COVERAGE SUMMARY

1. This auto insurance policy provides coverage for the vehicle described above, subject to the terms and conditions outlined below.

2. Coverage includes liability for damages or injuries caused to third parties, as well as uninsured motorist protection.

3. The policyholder is responsible for maintaining adequate proof of financial responsibility, including but not limited to, valid driver's licenses, car registration, and insurance cards.

DEDUCTIBLES AND LIMITS

1. Collision Deductible: $1000
2. Comprehensive Deductible: $1000
3. Liability Limits: $50,000/$100,000 per accident, aggregate limit of $100,000
4. Uninsured Motorist Limits: $50,000/$100,000 per person, aggregate limit of $100,000

EXCLUSIONS

1. Racing or speed contests involving the vehicle.
2. Commercial use of the vehicle.
3. Livery or ride-share use of the vehicle.
4. Intentional damage to the vehicle.

DEFINITIONS

1. Deductible: The amount that the policyholder must pay out-of-pocket for certain types of losses, such as collision and comprehensive claims.

2. Uninsured Motorist Protection: Coverage provided when an uninsured driver is involved in a collision with the policyholder's vehicle.

3. Coinsurance: A provision requiring the policyholder to share the cost of repairs or replacement with the insurer, typically specified as a percentage (e.g. 20%).

Note: The document assumes that coinsurance provisions are not explicitly mentioned in the provided facts, and therefore it is not included in the actual policy.
