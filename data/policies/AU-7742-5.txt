COVERAGE SUMMARY

This policy provides liability coverage for damages or injuries caused to others as a result of the Policyholder's use of the 2021 Toyota RAV4 vehicle. The policy also includes comprehensive coverage for damage to the vehicle, subject to the exclusions and deductibles outlined in this policy.

DEDUCTIBLES AND LIMITS

* Collision Deductible: $1,000
* Comprehensive Deductible: $500
* Liability Limits: $250,000/$500,000
* Uninsured Motorist Limits: $250,000/$500,000

EXCLUSIONS

The following types of losses are excluded from coverage under this policy:

* Racing or speed contests
* Commercial use of the vehicle
* Intentional damage
* Use of the vehicle to commit a crime

RIDERS

The following rider is included in this policy:

* Rental Reimbursement: $30/day, maximum 30 days

DEFINITIONS

For purposes of this policy:

* Deductible means the amount that the Policyholder must pay out-of-pocket before insurance coverage begins.
* Dwelling Coverage is not applicable to this auto insurance policy, as it refers to coverage for aPolicyholder's primary residence.
* Collision means damage to the vehicle caused by colliding with another object or vehicle, or other forms of impact.
* Coinsurance is not applicable in this policy.
