COVERAGE SUMMARY

This homeowners insurance policy provides coverage for the dwelling located at 5 Meadowbrook Drive, Lakeside, including damage from covered causes, as well as certain losses of personal property.

DEDUCTIBLES AND LIMITS

Schedule of Deductibles and Limits:

* Standard Deductible: $500.00 per occurrence
* Wind Hail Deductible: $1,000.00 per occurrence

* Dwelling Coverage Limit: $275,000.00
* Personal Property Coverage Limit: $137,500.00

EXCLUSIONS

The following are excluded from coverage under this policy:

* Flood damage
* Earth movement (including earthquake and land subsidence)
* Wear, tear, and gradual deterioration
* Mold, except when directly resulting from a covered water damage event
* Intentional acts by the insured

DEFINITIONS

1. Coinsurance: The percentage of loss shared between the Insured and the Insurance Company, as stated in this policy.

2. Dwelling Coverage: The amount of coverage provided for the dwelling located at 5 Meadowbrook Drive, Lakeside, including all permanent structures attached to it.

3. Personal Property Coverage: The amount of coverage provided for personal property owned by the Insured, excluding those items specifically listed under Exclusions.

4. Scheduled Property: Any specific item of personal property that is scheduled and declared in this policy, requiring special consideration during loss adjustments.
