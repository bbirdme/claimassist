COVERAGE SUMMARY

This health insurance policy provides coverage for eligible medical expenses incurred by the Policyholder during the policy period. The policy includes individual high-deductible coverage with a Health Savings Account (HSA)-eligible plan.

The policy covers certain medical services, including but not limited to primary care visits, specialist appointments, emergency room treatments, and hospital stays. However, it does not cover cosmetic procedures, experimental or investigational treatment, or injuries sustained during illegal activity.

DEDUCTIBLES AND LIMITS

Schedule of Deductibles and Limits:

* $3,500 (USD)
* Out-of-Pocket Maximum: $7,000 (USD)
* Coinsurance after deductible: 10%

EXCLUSIONS

The following services are excluded from coverage under this policy:

1. Cosmetic procedures
2. Experimental or investigational treatment
3. Injuries sustained during illegal activity

DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this policy, the following definitions apply:

* Deductible: The amount that the Policyholder must pay out-of-pocket for medical expenses before the insurance coverage kicks in.
* Coinsurance: The percentage of medical costs that the Policyholder pays after meeting their deductible. In this policy, coinsurance is 10% of eligible medical expenses.
* Out-of-Pocket Maximum (OOPM): The maximum amount that the Policyholder must pay out-of-pocket for medical expenses during the policy period. If the Policyholder exceeds the OOPM, the insurance coverage covers 100% of eligible medical expenses until the end of the policy period.
