HO-4471-9 HOMEOWNERS INSURANCE POLICY

DECLARATIONS

This policy is issued to David Chen (the "Insured") on November 15, 2025, and is effective until November 15, 2026. The Insured's address is 88 Maple Court, Riverside.

COVERAGE SUMMARY

This homeowners insurance policy provides coverage for the Insured's dwelling at the above address, as well as certain personal property owned by the Insured. The policy limits are as follows:

- Dwelling: $500,000
- Personal Property: $250,000

DEDUCTIBLES AND LIMITS

Schedule of Deductibles and Limits:

- Standard Deductible: $1,500
- Wind/Hail Deductible: $5,000

EXCLUSIONS

The following events and conditions are excluded from coverage under this policy:

Flood damage
Earth movement (including earthquake and land subsidence)
Wear, tear, and gradual deterioration
Mold, except when directly resulting from a covered water damage event
War and nuclear hazard
Intentional acts by the insured

DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this policy, the following definitions apply:

- Deductible: The amount that the Insured must pay out-of-pocket before the insurer will pay for a loss or claim.
- Dwelling Coverage: This term refers to the coverage provided for the Insured's primary residence, including the structure and its permanent fixtures and improvements.
- Coinsurance (not applicable in this policy): A provision requiring the Insured to pay a percentage of the loss or damage, with the insurer paying the remaining percentage.
