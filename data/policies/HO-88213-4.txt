HO-88213-4 INSURANCE POLICY

DECLARATIONS:

This policy is hereby issued to Maria Gutierrez, effective as of September 1, 2025, and expiring on September 1, 2026. The policy number assigned to this policy is HO-88213-4.

COVERAGE SUMMARY:

The Homeowners Insurance Policy issued by the undersigned provides coverage for the dwelling at 214 Birchwood Lane, Springfield, as well as personal property located therein. The policy's primary purpose is to protect Maria Gutierrez against unforeseen losses or damages resulting from various perils.

DEDUCTIBLES AND LIMITS:

Schedule of Deductibles and Limits:

* Standard Deductible: $1,000
* Wind/Hail Deductible: $2,500

Personal Property Limit: $175,000
 Dwelling Limit: $350,000

EXCLUSIONS:

The following perils or causes of loss are excluded from coverage under this policy:

Flood damage
Earth movement (including earthquake and land subsidence)
Wear, tear, and gradual deterioration
Mold, except when directly resulting from a covered water damage event
Intentional acts by the insured

DEFINITIONS:

For purposes of this insurance policy, the following terms are defined as follows:

* Deductible: The amount that Maria Gutierrez must pay out-of-pocket before the insurance coverage kicks in. For wind/hail-related losses, the deductible is $2,500; for standard causes of loss, the deductible is $1,000.
* Dwelling Coverage: The coverage provided by the policy to protect the physical structure of the home (i.e., 214 Birchwood Lane) against perils such as fire, lightning, windstorms, and theft.
* Coinsurance: Not applicable in this policy.
* Water Damage: Covered water damage is defined as damage resulting from a sudden, unintentional release or spill of water. Mold, however, is excluded unless it directly results from covered water damage.
