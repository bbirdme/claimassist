This health insurance policy provides coverage for Sofia Ramirez, an individual enrolled in the plan, from January 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026. The policy covers medical expenses incurred during this period, subject to the terms and conditions outlined below.

The following services are excluded from coverage under this policy:

• Cosmetic procedures

DEFINITIONS:

For the purposes of this insurance policy, the following terms have the following meanings:

1. Deductible (Deductible Amount): The amount that Sofia Ramirez must pay out-of-pocket for medical expenses before the insurer begins to share the cost of covered expenses.

2. Coinsurance Rate: The percentage rate at which the insurer shares the cost of covered expenses after the deductible amount has been met, as stated in this policy (20%).

3. Out-of-Pocket Maximum (OOPM): The maximum amount that Sofia Ramirez must pay for medical expenses during a specific period or year, excluding any amounts paid by the insurer.
