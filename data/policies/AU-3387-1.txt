DEFINITIONS

For purposes of this policy, the following terms shall be defined as follows:

1. Deductible: The amount that the Policyholder must pay out-of-pocket before the insurer begins paying coverage for a specific loss or claim.
2. Dwelling Coverage: Not applicable in this policy type, as it refers to coverage for the Policyholder's primary residence.
3. Collision: Damage to the vehicle caused by another vehicle, object, or person, resulting in physical contact between vehicles or objects.
